I'm Allen Ghanghas — a CIS grad building AI agents, writing when I can, and slowly putting down roots in Kelowna, BC.

## Background

I studied Computer Information Systems and spent a few years figuring out how software actually works in the real world, not just in textbooks. That gap turned out to be the most interesting part.

Right now I'm focused on building tools that use AI agents to automate the kinds of work that still feel annoyingly manual — the kind of task that's too messy for a script but too repetitive to keep doing by hand.

## Kelowna

I moved to Kelowna from elsewhere in Canada and I'm actively working through the permanent residency process. There's something grounding about choosing a specific place and deciding to stay. The Okanagan is beautiful and quieter than I expected, in a good way.

## What I'm Doing

- Building **Dior HQ** — my main project, an AI agent platform
- Writing about the intersection of technology and daily life
- Navigating immigration paperwork (it's a whole thing)
- Learning what it means to actually live somewhere versus just exist in it
